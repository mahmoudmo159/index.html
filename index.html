CASE STUDY • PROJECT 01

E-Commerce Analytics Dashboard

An interactive business dashboard designed to turn e-commerce data into clear performance insights across sales, revenue, profit, orders, customers, products and geographic performance.

Looker Studio E-Commerce KPI Data Visualization
THE BUSINESS CHALLENGE

Turning scattered business data into one clear view

The dashboard was designed around a common e-commerce reporting challenge: important information such as sales, revenue, profit, orders, customers, products and geographic performance needs to be viewed together instead of being checked separately.

Portfolio note: this challenge statement is written from the dashboard's scope and features. If you give me the client's exact real-world problem, I can replace it with the precise story.

THE SOLUTION

A single interactive analytics dashboard

The dashboard brings the key metrics into one visual workspace, making it easier to monitor performance, compare products and business areas, identify changes in results and move from raw data to actionable insights.

✓ Revenue & profit tracking ✓ Orders & customer analysis ✓ Product performance ✓ Geographic analysis ✓ Interactive KPIs & visuals
